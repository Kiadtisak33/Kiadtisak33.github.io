Last updated: 2 November 2026

English

Information we collect

  • Google Sign-In: when you sign in with Google we receive your Google account ID, name and email address from Google. We store only the Google account ID to recognize your account. We do not store your name, email address or Google password.
  • Guest play: a random key stored on your device. Our server stores only a hash of it.
  • Game data: the display name you choose, your characters, items, outfits, in-game currency and purchase history.
  • Session: a login token that expires after 30 days. Our server stores only a hash of it.

How we use it

Only to sign you in, save your game progress and run the game. We do not sell or share your information with third parties and we do not use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Email nonomxp81@gmail.com from the address of your Google account and we will delete your account and game data within 30 days. You can also remove the game's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
